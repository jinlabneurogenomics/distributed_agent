# S5E — shuffle controls by DEG strength

This panel uses the primary/load-bearing rows of
`final_local_signal_distribution.csv`.  The unit is a finding × associated
cell-type placement.  Strong, moderate, and low local-DEG strata are shown for
the unshuffled, within-screen shuffled, and out-of-screen shuffled arms; the
zero-DEG diagnostic stratum is intentionally omitted from the retained panel.

`finalize_distribution.py` is the archived generator and the copied
`final_flag_distribution_by_celltype_signal_primary` files are its original
full primary output.
