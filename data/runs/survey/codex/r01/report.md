# Methods and definitions

I treated a perturbation signature as the measured genes for one `gene_target` in one `group_name`. Genes were called signature members at Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted Wilcoxon `pvals_adj < 0.05` and `abs(logfoldchanges) >= 0.25`; discovery summaries also tracked the stricter `pvals_adj < 1e-6` and `abs(logfoldchanges) >= 0.5` tier. The complete table contained 745,758,369 rows; 99,882 rows were FDR < 0.05, and 93,807 also passed the log2 fold-change cutoff. I counted changed genes per target-cell signature, counted repeated same-direction hits across cell classes, and manually merged redundant signatures when multiple genes from the same pathway made the same biological point.

Literature searches used PubMed queries for each perturbed gene together with the recurrent downstream program, the affected process, disease synonyms, and cell-type context. I cite direct precedents when they existed, and otherwise cite the closest mechanistic precedent for the perturbed gene while treating the missing exact target-program-cell-type combination as evidence of novelty only, not as evidence of biological correctness.

Findings were first evidence-gated: signatures without FDR-significant downstream programs or with only target-gene knockdown were excluded. The 30 retained findings all have Support >= 3. Ranks were then assigned by expected value, using Priority as the dominant criterion, Support as the next criterion, and Novelty as the third; within the same score triplet, broader recurrence and cleaner pathway coherence broke ties.

The major limitations are that the parquet contains DE summaries rather than the raw guide-by-cell count matrix, so guide-level reproducibility and replicate-aware tests could not be recalculated. Wilcoxon p-values are single-nucleus statistics against pooled non-targeting controls and can be anti-conservative when cells from the same mouse are treated as independent. Acute AAV CRISPR in P16-P44 NeuN-enriched brain is not a germline, embryonic, aged, or glial screen; weak target mRNA depletion is expected to underestimate functional knockout, while transcript changes downstream of essential genes can reflect indirect stress or survival selection. Finally, a transcriptomic program shows a testable relationship but usually cannot distinguish direct transcriptional regulation from secondary signaling.

Core Idea: The dynactin subunit perturbation `Dctn1` upregulated SREBP/cholesterol genes rather than producing only a generic transport signature.
